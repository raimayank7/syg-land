Land units in Uttarakhand

Uttarakhand land measurement: 1 nali is 16 mutthi or 240 sq yards (0.02 hectare) in the hills; 1 bigha is 82.5 ft by 82.5 ft with 20 biswa in the plains.

Uttarakhand uses two systems: nali and mutthi in the hills, and bigha and biswa in the plains districts. Training material of the Uttarakhand Academy of Administration at Nainital gives both.

Hills

  • 1 nali = 16 mutthi
  • 1 nali = 240 square yards = 2,160 square feet
  • 1 nali = 0.02 hectare, so 50 nali make about a hectare

Plains

  • 1 bigha = 82.5 feet × 82.5 feet = 6,806.25 square feet
  • 1 bigha = 20 biswa = 0.0632 hectare
  • 1 acre = 6.4 bigha = 6 bigha 8 biswa

Between the two

One bigha is 3.15 nali. One acre is about 20.17 nali. One biswa, a twentieth of the bigha, is 340.31 square feet, or about 2.5 mutthi.

Rounding in the document

The nali is given as 240 square yards and also as 0.02 hectare. These differ slightly: 240 square yards is 200.67 square metres, and 0.02 hectare is 200 square metres. Our converter uses 240 square yards. For a registry, use the figure in your own record.

For a sale, a loan or a court paper, go by your own revenue record. Local units can differ between districts and between old and new records. The sizes here were read in the official documents named under Sources on 11 November 2026. This site is not a government site and gives no legal advice.

Questions people ask

What is 1 nali in square feet?

1 nali is 2,160 square feet (240 square yards). Source: the Uttarakhand Academy of Administration.

What is 1 mutthi in square feet?

1 mutthi is 135 square feet (15 square yards). Source: the Uttarakhand Academy of Administration.

What is 1 bigha (Uttarakhand) in square feet?

1 bigha (Uttarakhand) is 6,806.25 square feet (756.25 square yards). Source: the Uttarakhand Academy of Administration.
