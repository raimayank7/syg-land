Acre: 43,560 square feet, the unit that links all the others

1 acre is 43,560 square feet, 4,840 square yards or 0.4047 hectare. It equals 8 kanal, 40 guntha or 100 cents. Tables for Indian units and a visual guide.

Every local land unit in India was at some point tied to the acre by a survey or settlement. That makes the acre the easiest bridge between them: change your unit to acres, then acres to the unit you want.

The size

One acre is 4,840 square yards. That is 43,560 square feet, 4,046.86 square metres or 0.4047 hectare. A square acre has a side of about 208.7 feet.

An acre in local units

  • 8 kanal, or 160 marla (Haryana, Punjab). There the acre is called a killa or ghuman.
  • 40 guntha (Maharashtra, Karnataka, Andhra Pradesh, Telangana).
  • 100 cents or decimals (Kerala, Tamil Nadu, the east).
  • 3.025 bigha in Assam and Meghalaya.
  • 6.4 bigha, or about 20.17 nali, in Uttarakhand.
  • 1.6 bigha of the 165 foot jarib; 2.5 bigha of the 132 foot jarib (Rajasthan).

How big does it look?

A killa field in a consolidated Haryana village is 198 feet by 220 feet: that rectangle is exactly one acre. As a rough picture, an acre is a little more than half a full-size football pitch.

Acre and hectare

Government records are moving to hectares. One hectare is 2.471 acres, and one acre is 0.4047 hectare. To change acres to hectares quickly, multiply by 0.4; to change hectares to acres, multiply by 2.5. The quick way is off by about 1%.
