Hectare to acre

1 hectare is 2.471 acres. Table for 1 to 100 hectares in acres, the quick rule of 2.5, and what marginal and small holdings mean in acres.

Convert any number

Multiply hectares by 2.471. The quick way is to multiply by 2.5, which is about 1% too high. A holding of 2 hectares, the upper limit of a "small" farm in the agriculture census, is 4.94 acres.

The rule

Acres = hectares × 2.4711

What a hectare is

A hectare is a square of 100 metres by 100 metres: 10,000 square metres, or 1,07,639 square feet. It is the unit of the metric land record, the agriculture census and most government schemes.

Hectare, are and square metre

Metric records split the hectare into 100 are, and the are into 100 square metres. A figure such as 0.40.50 means 0 hectare, 40 are, 50 square metres, which is 4,050 square metres or 1.0008 acre. An entry of 0.40 hectare is 40 are, or 0.9884 acre.

One hectare in local units

  • 19.77 kanal, or 2.471 killa.
  • 98.84 guntha.
  • 247.1 cents.

Going back

Acres × 0.4047 = hectares. So 1 acre is 0.4047 hectare, 5 acres are 2.0234 hectares and 10 acres are 4.0469 hectares. When a form asks for hectares and your record is in a local unit, change to acres first, then to hectares, and keep four decimal places until the last step.

For a sale, a loan or a court paper, go by your own revenue record. Local units can differ between districts and between old and new records. The sizes here were read in the official documents named under Sources on 11 November 2026. This site is not a government site and gives no legal advice.

Questions people ask

How many acres is 1 hectare?

1 hectare is 2.4711 acres.

How do I change acres to hectares?

Multiply by 0.404686. 1 acre is 0.404686 hectares.
