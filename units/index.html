Land Units

Indian land units

23 units, each with an official source. Tap one for its size, tables and where it is used.

  • Square foot The everyday unit for plots and flats
  • Square yard (gaj) 9 square feet; called gaj in north India
  • Square metre The metric unit; 10.764 square feet
  • Are 100 square metres
  • Acre 4,840 square yards or 43,560 square feet
  • Hectare 10,000 square metres; 2.471 acres
  • Cent (decimal, dismil) One hundredth of an acre
  • Guntha (gunta) One fortieth of an acre
  • Kuncham 10 cents, Andhra Pradesh
  • Sarsahi (square karam) One karam by one karam, with a 66 inch karam
  • Marla 9 sarsahi
  • Kanal 20 marla; 8 kanal make an acre
  • Killa (ghuman) 8 kanal, the same as an acre
  • Bigha (165 ft jarib) Rajasthan, Shahjahani jarib: 165 ft by 165 ft
  • Bigha (132 ft jarib) Rajasthan, Gantari jarib: 132 ft by 132 ft
  • Bigha (Assam, Meghalaya) 3.025 bigha make an acre
  • Bigha (Haryana, before settlement) 4 bigha 16 biswa make an acre
  • Bigha (Uttarakhand) 82.5 ft by 82.5 ft; 6.4 bigha make an acre
  • Katha (Assam) 20 lessa
  • Lessa 144 square feet, Assam
  • Katha (Jharkhand) 66.89 square metres
  • Nali 240 square yards, Uttarakhand hills
  • Mutthi One sixteenth of a nali

That unit is not in our list. In the converter you can type the size of your own local unit.
