Hectare: 10,000 square metres, 2.471 acres

1 hectare is 10,000 square metres, 2.471 acres or 100 ares. See it in bigha, kanal, guntha, nali and cents, with tables and the quick rule of thumb.

The hectare is the official unit for land in India's records and statistics. A hectare is a square of 100 metres by 100 metres, so it holds 10,000 square metres. One hundredth of it, 100 square metres, is an are.

Where you meet it

Online land records in many states, crop insurance papers, farm scheme limits and land ceiling laws all state areas in hectares. Farm size classes are in hectares too: holdings below 1 hectare are called marginal and those from 1 to 2 hectares small in the agriculture census.

A hectare in local units

  • 2.471 acres.
  • 19.77 kanal, or about 395 marla.
  • 98.84 guntha.
  • 247.1 cents.
  • 7.475 bigha in Assam and Meghalaya.
  • About 50 nali, or 15.8 bigha, in Uttarakhand.

Reading "0-40-47" style entries

Metric land records in some states write hectare-are-square metre, for example 0-40-47. That is 0 hectare, 40 are and 47 square metres: 4,047 square metres in all, which is one acre.

Square metre to square feet

One square metre is 10.764 square feet. For a flat or a plot, multiply square metres by 10.764 to get square feet, or divide square feet by 10.764 to get square metres.
