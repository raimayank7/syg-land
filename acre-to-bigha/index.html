Acre to bigha

1 acre is 1.6 or 2.5 bigha in Rajasthan, 3.025 bigha in Assam and Meghalaya, 4.8 in old Haryana records and 6.4 in Uttarakhand. Table for 1 to 100 acres.

Open the converter

Divide 43,560 square feet by the square feet in your bigha. The Uttarakhand document states its answer directly: 1 acre = 6.4 bigha, or 6 bigha 8 biswa. The Meghalaya handbook gives 3.025 bigha.

When you need this

Bank, insurance and scheme forms usually ask for acres or hectares, while a seller or an elder in the village speaks in bigha. Going from acres to bigha lets you check that both are talking about the same piece of land.

Two and a half acres, five answers

  • 165 feet jarib: 4 bigha.
  • 132 feet jarib: 6.25 bigha.
  • Assam and Meghalaya: 7.5625 bigha.
  • Haryana, before settlement: 12 bigha.
  • Uttarakhand: 16 bigha.

Turning the decimal into biswa

A record does not say 6.4 bigha; it says 6 bigha 8 biswa. Multiply the part after the decimal point by 20 to get biswa: 0.4 × 20 = 8. For Haryana's old records, 4.8 bigha is 4 bigha 16 biswa. In Assam, multiply the decimal part by 5 to get katha instead: 3.025 bigha is 3 bigha and 0.125 katha, which is 2.5 lessa.

From hectares

Change hectares to acres first by multiplying by 2.4711, then read the table. One hectare is 3.954 bigha on the 165 feet jarib, 6.178 on the 132 feet jarib, 7.475 in Assam and 15.815 in Uttarakhand.

For a sale, a loan or a court paper, go by your own revenue record. Local units can differ between districts and between old and new records. The sizes here were read in the official documents named under Sources on 11 November 2026. This site is not a government site and gives no legal advice.

Questions people ask

How many bigha is 1 acre?

It depends on the bigha. Bigha (165 ft jarib): 1.6. Bigha (132 ft jarib): 2.5. Bigha (Assam, Meghalaya): 3.025. Bigha (Haryana, before settlement): 4.8. Bigha (Uttarakhand): 6.4.
