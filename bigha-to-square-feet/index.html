Bigha to square feet

1 bigha in square feet: 27,225 or 17,424 in Rajasthan, 14,400 in Assam and Meghalaya, 6,806.25 in Uttarakhand. Table for each official bigha.

Open the converter

A bigha is a square with one local chain as its side, so its square feet are the chain length multiplied by itself: 165 × 165, 132 × 132, 120 × 120 or 82.5 × 82.5. The Haryana figure comes from 4 bigha 16 biswa to the acre.

The same bigha in gaj and square metres

  • 165 feet jarib: 3,025 gaj, 2,529.29 square metres.
  • 132 feet jarib: 1,936 gaj, 1,618.74 square metres.
  • Assam and Meghalaya: 1,600 gaj, 1,337.8 square metres.
  • Haryana, before settlement: 1,008.33 gaj, 843.1 square metres.
  • Uttarakhand: 756.25 gaj, 632.32 square metres.

The smaller parts

Where a bigha is cut into 20 biswa, one biswa is a twentieth of these figures: 1,361.25 square feet on the 165 feet jarib, 871.2 on the 132 feet jarib, 453.75 in old Haryana records and 340.3125 in Uttarakhand. Assam divides differently: 5 katha of 2,880 square feet make its bigha, and each katha is 20 lessa of 144 square feet.

Checking a plot against a bigha

Measure the plot in feet and multiply to get square feet, then divide by the square feet of your own bigha. A field of 150 feet by 200 feet is 30,000 square feet. That is 1.1 bigha on the 165 feet jarib, 1.72 on the 132 feet jarib, 2.08 in Assam and 4.41 in Uttarakhand. The land is the same; only the name of the number changes.

For a sale, a loan or a court paper, go by your own revenue record. Local units can differ between districts and between old and new records. The sizes here were read in the official documents named under Sources on 11 November 2026. This site is not a government site and gives no legal advice.

Questions people ask

How many square feet is 1 bigha?

It depends on the bigha. Bigha (165 ft jarib): 27,225. Bigha (132 ft jarib): 17,424. Bigha (Assam, Meghalaya): 14,400. Bigha (Haryana, before settlement): 9,075. Bigha (Uttarakhand): 6,806.25.
