Guntha to square feet

1 guntha is 1,089 square feet, a square of 33 feet by 33 feet. Table for 1 to 100 guntha in square feet, with common plot sizes.

Convert any number

Multiply guntha by 1,089. The figure is the acre divided by 40. A handy picture: one guntha is a plot of 33 feet by 33 feet.

The rule

Square feet = guntha × 1,089

Questions people ask

How many square feet is 1 guntha (gunta)?

1 guntha (gunta) is 1,089 square feet.

How do I change square feet to guntha?

Multiply by 0.000918. 1 square foot is 0.000918 guntha.
