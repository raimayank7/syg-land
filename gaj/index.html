Gaj: one square yard, 9 square feet

1 gaj is one square yard: 9 square feet or 0.836 square metre. See 50, 100, 200 and 500 gaj plots in square feet, marla and square metres.

In Delhi, Haryana, Punjab, Uttar Pradesh and Rajasthan, house plots are sold by the gaj. A gaj is a yard, 3 feet long. When people say "100 gaj plot" they mean 100 square yards.

The size

A square yard is 3 feet by 3 feet, so 1 gaj = 9 square feet. In metric it is 0.836 square metre. Going the other way, 1 square metre is 1.196 gaj.

Gaj and marla

One marla is 30.25 gaj. So a 5 marla plot is 151.25 gaj, and a 100 gaj plot is 3.31 marla. Sellers often round these, so ask for the exact figure in the registry.

Length gaj and area gaj

Cloth is sold by the gaj as a length; land by the gaj as an area. A plot "30 gaj by 10 gaj" is 300 square gaj, which is 2,700 square feet, not 300 feet of anything. When a seller gives one number, check which he means.
