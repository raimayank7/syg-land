Bigha to acre

How many acres is 1 bigha? It depends on the bigha: 0.625, 0.4, 0.3306, 0.2083 or 0.1563 acre. Table for 1 to 100 bigha for each official size.

Open the converter

There is no single answer, because there is no single bigha. Find your region in the table. If it is not there, your bigha may be a different size again: see why the bigha differs.

The rule is the same for all: acres = bigha × (square feet in your bigha) ÷ 43,560.

Questions people ask

How many acres is 1 bigha?

It depends on the bigha. Bigha (165 ft jarib): 0.625. Bigha (132 ft jarib): 0.4. Bigha (Assam, Meghalaya): 0.3306. Bigha (Haryana, before settlement): 0.2083. Bigha (Uttarakhand): 0.1562.
