Square feet to gaj

To change square feet to gaj, divide by 9. Table for common areas from 450 to 9,000 square feet in gaj and square metres.

Convert any number

Divide square feet by 9. A gaj is a square yard, 3 feet by 3 feet. So 900 square feet is 100 gaj, and 1,800 square feet is 200 gaj.

The rule

Square yards (gaj) = square feet × 0.111111

Gaj as length and gaj as area

The word gaj is used for two things. A cloth merchant's gaj is a length of one yard, 3 feet. A property dealer's gaj is an area: one yard by one yard, 9 square feet. A "100 gaj plot" is 100 square yards, not a plot 100 yards long.

Working it out from the tape

Measure in feet, multiply, then divide by 9. A plot of 25 by 50 feet is 1,250 square feet, or 138.89 gaj. A plot of 30 by 60 feet is 1,800 square feet, exactly 200 gaj. If you measure in yards instead, the multiplication gives gaj directly: 10 yards by 20 yards is 200 gaj.

Gaj and square metres

Registries state area in square metres. One gaj is 0.8361 square metre, and one square metre is 1.196 gaj. So 100 gaj is 83.61 square metres, and 200 gaj is 167.23 square metres.

Gaj and marla

A revenue marla is 272.25 square feet, which is 30.25 gaj. A 100 gaj plot is therefore 3.31 marla, and a 250 gaj plot is 8.26 marla.

Flats

Flats are sold in square feet, and the figure may be carpet area, built-up area or super built-up area. Converting to gaj does not tell you which one you are being quoted. Ask, and get it in writing.

For a sale, a loan or a court paper, go by your own revenue record. Local units can differ between districts and between old and new records. The sizes here were read in the official documents named under Sources on 11 November 2026. This site is not a government site and gives no legal advice.

Questions people ask

How many square yards (gaj) is 1 square foot?

1 square foot is 0.111111 square yards (gaj).

How do I change square yards (gaj) to square feet?

Multiply by 9. 1 square yard (gaj) is 9 square feet.
