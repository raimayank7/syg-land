Nali to square feet

1 nali is 240 square yards or 2,160 square feet in Uttarakhand. Table for 1 to 100 nali in square feet, acres and hectares.

Convert any number

Multiply nali by 2,160. In mutthi: 16 mutthi make a nali, so each mutthi is 135 square feet.

The rule

Square feet = nali × 2,160

The nali in other units

  • 240 square yards.
  • 200.67 square metres.
  • 20.17 nali make an acre, and 49.83 nali make a hectare.

A plot of 40 feet by 54 feet is exactly one nali.

Nali and mutthi together

Hill records of Uttarakhand write area as nali and mutthi, with 16 mutthi to the nali. Work each part and add. For 3 nali 8 mutthi: 3 × 2,160 plus 8 × 135, which is 6,480 + 1,080 = 7,560 square feet. Eight mutthi is half a nali, so the same plot is 3.5 nali.

Nali and the Uttarakhand bigha

The plains districts of the state use the bigha of 6,806.25 square feet. One such bigha is 3.151 nali, and one nali is 0.3174 bigha. People who move between hill and plain land need this more than any other conversion.

Going back

Square feet ÷ 2,160 = nali. For a house site of 1,500 square feet that is 0.694 nali, or about 11.1 mutthi. Square metres ÷ 200.67 = nali.

For a sale, a loan or a court paper, go by your own revenue record. Local units can differ between districts and between old and new records. The sizes here were read in the official documents named under Sources on 11 November 2026. This site is not a government site and gives no legal advice.

Questions people ask

How many square feet is 1 nali?

1 nali is 2,160 square feet.

How do I change square feet to nali?

Multiply by 0.000463. 1 square foot is 0.000463 nali.
