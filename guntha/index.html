Guntha: one fortieth of an acre, 1,089 square feet

1 guntha is one fortieth of an acre: 1,089 square feet or 121 square yards. Used in Maharashtra, Karnataka, Andhra Pradesh and Telangana. Tables and examples.

The guntha, also written gunta, is the small unit of farm and plot land in the Deccan. The land records data standard records it for Andhra Pradesh and Karnataka as 40 guntha to the acre. It is also the everyday unit in Maharashtra and Telangana.

The size

An acre is 43,560 square feet. One fortieth of that is 1,089 square feet, which is a square of 33 feet by 33 feet, or 121 square yards. In metric it is about 101.17 square metres; the data standard rounds this to "100 square metres, around 1,100 square feet".

Acre and guntha together

Records in these states write an area as acres and guntha, for example 2 acres 15 guntha, often shown as 2-15 or 2.15. Take care with the second form: 2.15 here does not mean 2.15 acres. It means 2 acres and 15 guntha, which is 2 + 15/40 = 2.375 acres.

Guntha and cent

Both divide the acre, one into 40 parts and the other into 100. So 1 guntha is 2.5 cents, and 1 cent is 0.4 guntha. A plot of 6 guntha is 15 cents, or 6,534 square feet.

Worked example

A layout plot is 30 feet by 40 feet, that is 1,200 square feet. In guntha it is 1,200 ÷ 1,089 = 1.10 guntha.
