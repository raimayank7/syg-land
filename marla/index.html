Marla: 272.25 square feet, 30.25 gaj

1 marla is 9 sarsahi, 272.25 square feet or 30.25 square yards (gaj) with the 66 inch karam. 20 marla make a kanal. Table for 1 to 100 marla and plot sizes.

House plots across Haryana and Punjab are sold by the marla: a 5 marla house, a 10 marla plot. One marla is 9 sarsahi, and a sarsahi is a square of one karam by one karam.

The arithmetic

With the 66 inch karam of the land records standard, a karam is 5.5 feet. A sarsahi is 5.5 × 5.5 = 30.25 square feet. Nine of them make a marla: 9 × 30.25 = 272.25 square feet. Divide by 9 again and you get 30.25 square yards, so one marla is 30.25 gaj. Twenty marla make a kanal.

Market marla and revenue marla

In some property markets, sellers count a marla as fewer square feet than the revenue marla of 272.25. That makes a plot sound bigger in marlas than it is. Always ask for the area in square feet or square yards, and check it against the registry.

Karam and sarsahi

The karam is a length, about a double pace. The sarsahi is the smallest area unit in the chain. Records made before settlement in Haryana used a shorter karam of 57.157 inches, with biswansi, biswa and bigha instead of sarsahi, marla and kanal.

Questions people ask

How many square feet is 1 marla?

1 marla is 272.25 square feet, which is 30.25 square yards or 25.29 square metres. Source: the Government of India land records data standard.

How many square feet is 1 sarsahi (square karam)?

1 sarsahi (square karam) is 30.25 square feet, which is 3.3611 square yards or 2.81 square metres. Source: the Government of India land records data standard.
