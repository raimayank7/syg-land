Cent to square feet

1 cent (decimal) is 435.6 square feet. Table for 1 to 100 cents in square feet, with 3, 5 and 10 cent house plots worked out.

Convert any number

Multiply cents by 435.6. The same sum works for the decimal or dismil of eastern India, which is the same hundredth of an acre.

The rule

Square feet = cents (decimals) × 435.6

Questions people ask

How many square feet is 1 cent (decimal, dismil)?

1 cent (decimal, dismil) is 435.6 square feet.

How do I change square feet to cents (decimals)?

Multiply by 0.002296. 1 square foot is 0.002296 cents (decimals).
