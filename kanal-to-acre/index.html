Kanal to acre

8 kanal make 1 acre, so 1 kanal is 0.125 acre. Table for 1 to 100 kanal in acres, and how farm land is written in killa, kanal and marla.

Convert any number

Divide kanal by 8. Farm land is usually said in killa and kanal together: 20 kanal is 2 killa 4 kanal, which is 2.5 acres.

The rule

Acres = kanal × 0.125
