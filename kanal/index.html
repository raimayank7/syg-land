Kanal: 20 marla, one eighth of an acre

1 kanal is 20 marla, 5,445 square feet or 605 square yards, and 8 kanal make one acre (killa). Tables, the official chain of units and a worked example.

The kanal is the working unit of land in Haryana and Punjab, and it is also used in Jammu and Kashmir. Farm land is spoken of in killas (acres) and kanals; house plots in kanals and marlas.

The chain of units

The Government of India land records data standard gives this chain for Haryana after settlement:

  • 1 karam = 66 inches (5.5 feet)
  • 1 sarsahi = 1 karam × 1 karam = 30.25 square feet
  • 9 sarsahi = 1 marla = 272.25 square feet
  • 20 marla = 1 kanal = 5,445 square feet
  • 8 kanal = 1 acre, also called a ghuman or killa

Each step is exact, so a kanal is exactly one eighth of an acre: 43,560 ÷ 8 = 5,445 square feet, or 605 square yards (gaj).

Killa: the field you can see

In consolidated villages each acre field is laid out as a rectangle of 36 karam by 40 karam, which is 198 feet by 220 feet. That rectangle is one killa. This is why farmers count in killas: you can stand at one corner and see the whole unit.

Worked example

A plot is written as 2 kanal 7 marla. In marla that is 2 × 20 + 7 = 47 marla. In square feet it is 47 × 272.25 = 12,795.75. In square yards it is 12,795.75 ÷ 9 = 1,421.75 gaj.

Is a kanal the same everywhere?

The 5,445 square feet kanal follows from the 66 inch karam. Where older records used a different karam, the old kanal differs. For a sale or a loan, go by the karam your own revenue record names.

Questions people ask

How many square feet is 1 kanal?

1 kanal is 5,445 square feet, which is 605 square yards or 505.86 square metres. Source: the Government of India land records data standard.

How many square feet is 1 killa (ghuman)?

1 killa (ghuman) is 43,560 square feet, which is 4,840 square yards or 4,046.86 square metres. Source: the Government of India land records data standard.
